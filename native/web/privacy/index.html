Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains how KOMNIVO handles information when you use our mobile application, web experience and related community and tournament features.

In plain language: KOMNIVO uses account and profile information to run your account; stores community, chat and tournament content that you submit; and uses Firebase Cloud Messaging device tokens to deliver notifications. We do not sell personal information.

1. Who operates KOMNIVO

KOMNIVO is an independent esports community platform and developer-led project. For privacy questions, data requests or concerns, contact jarahul989@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and sign-in information: account identifier and, when supplied by Google sign-in, basic profile details such as your name, email address and profile image.
  • Player profile information: username, display name, avatar, bio, region and other details you choose to add.
  • Community and communication content: communities you create or join, membership and team information, messages, reactions, attachments, pins and related metadata.
  • Competition information: event registrations, team rosters, match check-ins, schedules, results, disputes, evidence and organiser decisions.
  • Notification information: your notification preferences and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration token, which is a device/app identifier used to route push notifications. The app may also record platform and update timestamps.
  • Support and safety information: information you include when contacting support or submitting a moderation report.
  • Optional game-profile lookups: when this feature is enabled and you request it, a game UID and selected region may be sent to an unofficial third-party lookup provider to retrieve public game-profile information.

3. Google sign-in and Google data

KOMNIVO uses Google OAuth through Supabase Auth to authenticate your KOMNIVO account. We may receive basic account information that Google makes available in the sign-in flow, such as your name, email address and profile photo. KOMNIVO does not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Google Contacts, Calendar or other personal Google content as part of ordinary sign-in.

Separately, the platform operator configures a server-side Google OAuth credential with the narrow https://www.googleapis.com/auth/firebase.messaging scope to send FCM notifications for the KOMNIVO Firebase project. That credential is used only by the server-side notification function to obtain short-lived access tokens and call the FCM send endpoint. It is stored as a Supabase Edge Function secret, is not included in the mobile app, and is not used to read end-user Google content.

4. How we use information

  • To create and secure accounts and provide sign-in.
  • To display profiles and operate communities, teams, channels and messaging.
  • To administer events, registrations, match workflows, results, disputes and standings.
  • To deliver notifications and apply your notification preferences.
  • To operate optional LiveOps tools, respond to support requests, prevent abuse and protect platform integrity.
  • To maintain, debug and improve the service, and meet applicable legal obligations.

5. Android screen capture and OCR

The optional LiveOps assistant may use Android MediaProjection. Screen capture starts only after you grant the Android system permission. Text recognition is performed on-device using Google ML Kit. The normal preview flow does not automatically upload raw screen frames. If you choose to submit a match observation, result or related workflow item, the information you submit (which may include extracted text or fields) can be stored in the KOMNIVO backend for that workflow.

6. Voice rooms and third-party game services

Where voice rooms are enabled, real-time audio communications are processed through LiveKit to provide the room service. Availability depends on the platform configuration. Optional game UID lookup may use the Free Fire Community API or another configured provider. Such a provider is independent of KOMNIVO; game lookup results are not official account-ownership verification. We do not represent that KOMNIVO is affiliated with Garena or any game publisher.

7. Service providers and sharing

We use service providers to operate the platform, including Supabase for authentication, database and private file storage; Google/Firebase for OAuth and push messaging; Cloudflare Pages for the public website; and LiveKit when voice features are enabled. Optional game-profile lookup may involve the configured third-party provider.

We do not sell personal information. We share information only as needed to provide a feature you use, with the service providers described above, with other users according to the visibility and membership settings of the feature (for example, community messages and public profiles), or when required to protect users, enforce our terms or comply with law.

8. Storage locations and international processing

The connected Supabase project currently uses the Singapore region. Our providers may process or store information in other locations as part of delivering their services. Where applicable, we take reasonable steps to use reputable providers and protect information in transit and at rest.

9. Retention and deletion requests

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations and preserve legitimate platform records. Retention periods can vary by data type and provider. To request access, correction or deletion of your account information, email jarahul989@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and describe the request. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Some records may need to be retained where the law permits or requires it; residual copies may remain in provider backups until their normal rotation.

10. Security

We use access controls, private storage, database row-level security and server-side secrets as part of our security design. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your account and device secure and for reporting suspected unauthorized access.

11. Children and age requirements

KOMNIVO is not designed for children below the minimum age permitted to use online services under the laws that apply to them. If you are a minor, use the service only where permitted by applicable law and with any parent or guardian authorization required by that law. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information improperly.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as features, providers or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date on this page. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or through the contact details associated with your account.

13. Contact

Privacy and data requests: jarahul989@gmail.com.
